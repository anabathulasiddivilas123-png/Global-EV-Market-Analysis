## 📊 Project Overview
Analyzed comprehensive global EV data from **2010-2024** to understand:
- Market leaders and emerging opportunities
- Technology adoption (BEV vs PHEV)
- Role of charging infrastructure in adoption
- Real environmental impact

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Python & EDA** - Data Cleaning & Analysis
- **MySQL** - Data Querying & Management
- **Power BI** - Interactive Dashboard & Storytelling

## 💡 Key Insights
- China dominates global EV sales & stock
- BEV technology is overtaking PHEV as the future
- Strong correlation between charging network growth and EV sales
- Significant CO2 emissions avoided & oil displacement
